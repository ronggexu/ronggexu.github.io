# Rongge Xu

Personal academic website of Rongge Xu, Postdoctoral Fellow at the Yau Mathematical Sciences Center, Tsinghua University.

My research focuses on higher category theory and mathematical physics, particularly higher Morita theory and topological orders. I also work on Lean-based formalization and AI for mathematics.
