I am a postdoctoral fellow at the Yau Mathematical Sciences Center, Tsinghua University.

I work on higher category theory and its applications to mathematical physics, particularly higher Morita theory and topological orders. I also work on Lean-based formalization and AI for mathematics.

27 August 2026 · New preprint

2-Morita Theory of E2-Algebras and Module Categories

With Holiverse Yang. We study topological orders through higher Morita categories and connect their algebraic formulation to module categories. The framework also explains how bi-bimodules describe local and confined modules and the defects produced by condensation.
